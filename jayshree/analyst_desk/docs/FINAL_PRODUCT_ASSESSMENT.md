## Product objective and scope

Analyst Desk prepares inspectable company-comparison research from annual SEC filings. Its contribution is evidence preparation: retrieving disclosures, reconciling reported inputs, calculating financial comparisons, drafting supported findings and presenting them for analyst review. Forecasts, valuation and investment decisions remain outside this product scope.

The adopted historical scope is 15 companies in Technology and Finance over FY2022–FY2024: 45 company-years of intended coverage. Company coverage does not establish that every metric is available. Operating margin and bank pre-tax margin remain different tasks, and differing issuer fiscal years require explicit dates and a comparison qualification.

The final product baseline includes the problem statement, user workflow, supported scope, bank metrics, fiscal periods and units, filing versions, evidence and uncertainty, acceptance and publication, memory and privacy, development reference answers and frontend messages. Those specifications define required behaviour; their existence is not proof of universal implementation.

## Practitioner input and its meaning

Charles Cheng's written feedback describes reading news, filings and reports, updating an Excel earnings and valuation model, then revising the investment thesis. Information collection, updating and communication constrain coverage. He emphasises actual findings relative to expectations and requires assertions to trace to original documents or figures rather than trusting raw generated output.

The resulting product choices are explicit: preserve sources; distinguish movement from cause; keep expectations separate from actual trends; and measure total research plus review effort. The documented example of revenue rising from 80 to 90 while missing a prior expectation of 100 shows why 12.50% growth and a 10.00% expectations shortfall can coexist.

This feedback establishes practitioner engagement and informs the workflow. It does not establish that Charles inspected this application version, reviewed the exact selected note, approved the policies or endorsed the product. No such endorsement is claimed. Reported informal disclosure-comparison observations are useful checkpoints, not measured evidence of predictive investment performance.

## Final disposition of the selected research note

The assessed note is **Operating Margin Trends and Risk Disclosure Changes for Microsoft and Alphabet (FY2022-FY2024)**, run `comparison-20261109-c2`, on historical application revision `42e2298a26bffb918ac8320543f6bc0d9591405c`. Its canonical exact-note hash is `93ec0a18e89bff4b2ff51a4f70ecd1f7c9c5b017e2a25ca9a7c987e0b0b8f992`. It is not a result from latest Main.

The separately calculated cached-fact review supports these six values:

| Company | FY2022 | FY2023 | FY2024 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Microsoft operating margin | 42.06% | 41.77% | 44.64% |
| Alphabet operating margin | 26.46% | 27.42% | 32.11% |

For example, Microsoft FY2024 is `109433 / 245122 × 100 = 44.64%` after final rounding. Issuer, concepts, accessions, USD units and matching annual dates were checked in the cached records. Complete statement-cell interpretation, original section boundaries, amendments, non-reliance and current source eligibility were outside that bounded pass.

The note remains **not accepted for publication as submitted** for the following substantive reasons:

- Microsoft's FY2023 productivity-cost and operating-margin causal explanation lacks adequate captured support. Correct revenue and cost facts do not by themselves complete the causal bridge.
- Microsoft's FY2024 gross-margin dollars grew 17%, as the captured source states. The statement needs its dollar-growth meaning, rather than an interpretation as a 17-percentage-point margin-rate increase.
- Alphabet's supported FY2024 Services and Cloud drivers need distinct scopes and offsets. Services compensation fell; Cloud compensation rose. The captured sources do not establish the claimed G&A reduction or FY2023 workforce/cost-management explanation.
- Present FY2024 risk topics are supported, but comparable earlier topic coverage is insufficient for New, Added, Expanded or Unchanged classifications. FY2022 risk coverage is not accounted for in the final cited packet.
- Fiscal comparison qualifications and limitations need clearer presentation, and negative causal-link statements must be limited to the reviewed evidence.

The original automatic result is preserved: **7/7 detected numerical items, 2/8 selected judge lines supported, six issues and `check.ok=false` after two revisions**. Eight judge lines are not eight atomic assertions. Manual decomposition of 33 narrative assertions does not replace this failed result or supply independent human gold labels.

Important critic objections are explained by evidence assembly. The Microsoft 17% sentence begins at zero-based character offset 1067, outside the judge's first-900-character source excerpt. Alphabet's relevant FY2024 source is the third citation on its driver line, outside the first-two-citation limit. These are evidence-supply defects, not proof that the full captured sources lacked the supported facts. Genuine wording and comparative-evidence defects still remain.

The assessment has not edited the note, granted final approval or published it. An eligible future version would need source-backed corrections, agreed scope, complete fresh review and its own exact-note approval. That is a future product action, not a condition for treating this assessment document as complete.
