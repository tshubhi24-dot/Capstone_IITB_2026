This version is complete for submission. Additional results or implementation changes belong in an identified later version.

## What the product is intended to do

Analyst Desk helps an equity research analyst prepare a cited company-comparison note from annual filings. It retrieves relevant disclosures, associates reported figures with their sources, calculates ratios in code, compares years and companies, reviews claims and presents the exact note for a human decision before publication.

The supported scope is 15 companies in Technology and Finance across FY2022–FY2024. Fiscal-year differences, metric definitions, missing data and incomplete evidence must remain visible. Forecasts, valuation, news/consensus feeds and investment recommendations are outside the agreed core scope.

Practitioner workflow feedback informs the design: evidence gathering, model updates and communication constrain company coverage, while assertions must trace to original documents or figures. A useful note must distinguish an observed movement, an expectations surprise and a supported explanation. The feedback is not claimed as prototype endorsement.
