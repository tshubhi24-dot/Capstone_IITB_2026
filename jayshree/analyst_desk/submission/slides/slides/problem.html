The user and the problem

Who

An equity research analyst at an asset manager

Reads 10-Ks, extracts numbers, computes ratios, compares peers and years, tracks risk-factor changes, then writes a note.

The stake

One wrong number undermines the whole note

Language models mistype figures, mix fiscal years and units. So the model never writes a number: code does.

Scope: 15 companies (tech and banks), FY2022–FY2024, SEC EDGAR filings and XBRL facts.

The brief: a single wrong number destroys trust in a research note. Our design keeps arithmetic and figures out of the model entirely. The model chooses which figure to mention and explains it; code fetches, computes and checks it.
