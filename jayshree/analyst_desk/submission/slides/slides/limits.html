Limits, and what a pilot needs

Stated limits

  • Fixed corpus: 15 companies, FY2022–FY2024
  • Uncited lines are flagged, not judged
  • Typed names, no sign-in
  • No correction or withdrawal of a published note

Before a supervised pilot

  • Sign-in and per-analyst access
  • A sandbox container per call
  • A trained injection classifier
  • Measured analyst time saved

The model proposes; code and people decide.
