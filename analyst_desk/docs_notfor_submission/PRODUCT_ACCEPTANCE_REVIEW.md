## Step 4 Check the business explanations

**Microsoft FY2023: needs narrower wording.** The source supports Cloud cost-of-revenue growth and segment revenue growth. It does not establish that productivity-segment costs caused the operating-margin decline. Retain the observed ratio movement separately; remove or source the missing cost and causal explanation. Revenue and gross-profit movements alone do not fully reconcile an operating-margin rate.

**Microsoft FY2024: retain supported facts with attribution.** The captured MD&A supports revenue growth across the named segments. Management also reports gross-margin dollars growing by 17%. Clarify the dollar-amount meaning; this is not a 17-percentage-point operating-margin increase. Do not claim a complete operating-expense or margin bridge without its evidence.

**Alphabet FY2023: missing evidence.** The cited MD&A introduction does not establish the draft's workforce-reduction and cost-management explanation. Remove those statements or supply their actual supporting passages. Missing captured support does not prove that the real-world events never occurred.

**Alphabet FY2024: separate the segments.** Services compensation reduction is supported; Cloud compensation costs increased. The draft's combined wording needs scope and offsets, while its general-and-administrative reduction is unverified. Segment operating-income drivers do not alone establish the full consolidated margin explanation.

**Example of safer wording:** “Management attributes Services operating-income growth primarily to revenue growth, with compensation reduction contributing. Cloud growth was partly offset by infrastructure and compensation costs.” Keep the code-derived consolidated margin trend separate unless a complete bridge is supplied.
